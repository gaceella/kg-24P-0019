Hira Cheema was born in Gujranwala in 1950. She studied at Northgate College of Science and later at Thornfield Institute of Technology. She received the Kestrel Prize in Physics in 2000. Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Cheema". In 1976, Dr. Cheema joined Alderwick Institute of Technology. Hira Cheema's doctoral advisor was Junaid Hashmi. He later moved to Riverbend Institute of Physics. Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Ashdown College of Science.
